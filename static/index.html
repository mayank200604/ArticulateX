LEVEL 1 EXPECTATIONS

FLUENCY FOCUS

Acknowledge the following before proceeding with this session.

  • 01. The AI will never simply agree with your point, but it will stay polite and encouraging while challenging you.
  • 02. This level has zero forced interruptions — you can speak at your own pace.
  • 03. Use this level to build fluency without pressure.
⚠

LEVEL 2 EXPECTATIONS

MODERATE PRESSURE

Acknowledge the following before proceeding with this session.

  • 01. The AI will never simply agree with your point, and it will push back competitively — but fairly.
  • 02. Expect interruptions roughly 1 in 4 times a rule is triggered — noticeably more pressure than Level 1.
  • 03. Stay clear and concise; this level rewards holding your ground under moderate pressure.
⚠

LEVEL 3 WARNING

EYE. SER. AI

Acknowledge the following operational constraints before proceeding with this session.

  • 01. It won't end on its own — you decide when to stop.
  • 02. The AI will never concede a point, no matter how strong your argument is.
  • 03. Lead with your point immediately. Wandering before you get there will trigger an interrupt.
